Mission Values

Values

Our Values

  • Everything is connected to everything.

    Whether obvious or hidden, strong or faint, everything is related to everything else. We approach our work, our decisions and our lives with the responsibility this brings.

  • Small moves lead to big results.

    We work knowing that even small steps in the right direction can pay off enormously. Big or small, we feel responsible for taking the steps we believe are right, and we feel the excitement of waiting to see what they bring.

  • We separate a person's worth from their economic value.

    As cognitive automation takes over the work people do, their value inside a company may seem to shrink. While discovering the new way of working, we respect and trust people, and we care deeply about finding their renewed, valuable place in it.

  • Technology is a tool, and a tool is technology.

    We don't see technology as a goal in itself; it is worth as much as the need it serves. At the same time, a pen, a spreadsheet or a habit is technology too: it doesn't have to be electronic or smart.

  • Cognitive automation creates abundance.

    When routine thinking work is handed over to machines, time, attention and creativity are freed up, and people can focus on what really matters.

  • Change is inevitable, and it is not new.

    From the printing press to electricity, humanity has lived through many great transformations, and cognitive automation is one more. Rather than resisting change, we learn from the past, meet it calm, curious and prepared, and make the most of what it brings.

Our Operating Principles

  • We are not afraid of mistakes; we fix them quickly.

    Making mistakes is a natural part of trying new things and learning. Instead of hiding them, we talk about them openly, understand why they happened and fix them fast. This builds trust and keeps the same mistake from happening again.

  • We design with care.

    We create aesthetic, harmonious and inspiring designs in which every part is carefully chosen and carefully connected to every other.

  • We create beauty.

    We care not only that our work works, but that it is beautiful. Careful design, clear writing and a tidy process are a form of respect for the people who use them, and they naturally raise the quality of the work.

  • We create ease.

    Making the complex simple is at the heart of what we do. We measure a solution by how much easier it makes people's lives, and we remove needless steps so that energy goes to work that truly adds value.

  • Execution is done playfully.

    We choose to approach even serious work with curiosity and a sense of play. Playfulness makes it easier to experiment, explore and stay open to new ideas; it keeps teams energized and makes room for the most creative solutions.

  • It is people who direct and evaluate.

    However advanced automation becomes, people always set the direction and judge the results. Machines suggest and speed up the work; people make the final call and own it. Every system we build keeps people in control and stays transparent.
